Article Title: Kishor Sagar Lake: Kota’s Mirror of History

Kishor Sagar is a historic lake in the heart of Kota, associated with the city’s royal landscape. Its waters and surrounding promenades offer a calm counterpoint to the bustle of the city and frame views of nearby heritage landmarks.

Attractions and architectural highlights: Lake views, waterfront walks, the island palace Jagmandir and evening photography.
