Dharmrajeshwara: A Monolithic Wonder Carved from Rock
Location: Shree Dharmrajeshwara Temple Place: Chandwasa Genre: Architectural
Near Chandwasa, Dharmrajeshwara is a remarkable rock-cut temple complex associated with early medieval religious
traditions. Carved from natural rock, it demonstrates careful planning and craftsmanship. Its imagery and worship
traditions connect Shaiva and Vaishnava forms.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Monolithic construction, rock-cut passages, shrine spaces, sculptural
details and the integration of temple and natural rock.
