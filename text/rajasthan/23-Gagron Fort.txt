Gagron Fort: The Water Fort of Rajasthan
Location: Gagron Fort Place: Gagron Genre: Fort
Gagron Fort is dramatically positioned at the meeting of the Ahu and Kali Sindh rivers. Its natural water barriers and
fortified walls make it a distinctive example of a strategic hill-and-water fort. The fort is part of the UNESCO-listed Hill
Forts of Rajasthan.
Attractions and architectural highlights: River confluence, ramparts, gateways, defensive architecture and
panoramic views.
