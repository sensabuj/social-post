Jhalawar Government Museum: A Window into Hadoti’s Past
Location: Government Museum Place: Jhalwar Genre: Museum
The Government Museum in Jhalawar preserves archaeological and cultural material from the surrounding region. Its
collections provide context for the temples, forts and historic settlements encountered across Hadoti.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Sculptures, archaeological artefacts and regional history collections.
