description: The Chhatri of Maharaja Yashwant Rao Holkar stands in Bhanpura, Mandsaur district, Madhya Pradesh. Yashwant Rao Holkar I was a celebrated ruler of the Holkars of Indore who died in 1811, and the memorial raised in his honour is built in stone in the Maratha style, with a raised plinth, fine pillars and a carved dome. The monument stands beside the river and is a quiet reminder of Holkar history.
