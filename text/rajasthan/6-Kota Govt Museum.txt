Article Title: Kota Government Museum: Stories Preserved in Art and Artefacts

Housed in the historic Brij Vilas Palace complex, Kota Government Museum preserves objects that illuminate the region’s royal, artistic and cultural past. Its collections help visitors understand the Kota school of painting and the material culture of the former princely state.

Attractions and architectural highlights: Sculptures, miniature paintings, manuscripts, weapons, coins and the historic palace setting.
