description: Shree Dharmrajeshwara Temple is a rock-cut temple complex at Chandwasa in Mandsaur district, Madhya Pradesh. Carved out of a single hillside, it is often compared in concept to the better known cave temples of Maharashtra, with a main shrine, pillared mandapa and related cave cells, all hewn from stone. Experts place it in the early medieval period, and the monolithic carving makes it a remarkable and little-visited site.
