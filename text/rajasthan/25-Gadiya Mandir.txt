Gadiya Mandir: A Quiet Chapter of Local Sacred Heritage
Location: Gadiya Mandir Place: Artu Genre: Architectural
Gadiya Mandir is one of the lesser-known religious stops in the Artu area. Its value lies in its place within the rural
sacred landscape of southeastern Rajasthan, where local shrines preserve traditions beyond major pilgrimage centres.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Temple setting, local worship traditions and surviving architectural details.
