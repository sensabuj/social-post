Nagar Sagar Kund: Bundi’s Water Architecture in Stone
Location: Nagar Sagar Kund Place: Bundi Genre: Architectural
Nagar Sagar Kund is part of Bundi’s celebrated tradition of water architecture. Stepwells and kunds were designed to
collect and conserve water while serving as important social and architectural spaces. Their stairways and stonework
demonstrate how utility and beauty were combined.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Stepped access, stone construction, water-harvesting design and
architectural symmetry.
