Bhand Devara Temple: The Khajuraho of Rajasthan
Location: Bhand Devara Temple Place: Ramgarh Genre: Architectural
Bhand Devara Temple at Ramgarh is among the region’s most celebrated medieval shrines. Often compared with the
temples of Khajuraho for its sculptural richness, it is dedicated to Shiva and reflects the sophistication of temple
architecture in the Hadoti region.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Shikhara, carved exterior panels, sculptural figures, mandapa and sacred
setting.
