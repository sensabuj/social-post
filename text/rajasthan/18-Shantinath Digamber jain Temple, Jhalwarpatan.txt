description: The Shantinath Digamber Jain Temple is in Jhalrapatan, Jhalawar district, Rajasthan, dedicated to Shantinath, the sixteenth Jain Tirthankara. The temple tradition dates back to the medieval period, and it has a finely carved entrance, pillared halls, and an image of Shantinath within the sanctum. Together with the Sun Temple nearby, it shows how deeply Hindu and Jain traditions have shared this town.
