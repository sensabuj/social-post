description: Mini Goa is a popular nickname for the sandy lakeshore on the Gandhi Sagar reservoir near Bhanpura, Madhya Pradesh. When the lake recedes after the monsoon, broad stretches of sand and shallow water make the place feel like a coast, with sunsets, picnics and water activities at certain times. There is no heritage architecture here, but the Chambal backwaters and island views add to its charm.
