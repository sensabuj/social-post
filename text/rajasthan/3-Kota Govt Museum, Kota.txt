description: The Government Museum in Kota is housed in the Brij Vilas Palace within the Garh Palace complex. It is a window into Hadoti's royal past, with collections of Kota school miniature paintings, sculptures from nearby temple sites, weaponry, coins and manuscripts. The palace setting adds painted halls, carved balconies and courtyards to the experience, and the miniatures, famous for hunting and court scenes, are the real highlight.
