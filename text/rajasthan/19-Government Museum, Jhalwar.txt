description: The Government Museum in Jhalawar is one of Rajasthan's older museums, set up in the early 20th century under the Jhalawar rulers. Its galleries hold stone sculptures from Hadoti temples, Gupta- and medieval-period images, inscriptions, coins, manuscripts, weapons and paintings. The building has a characteristic stately colonial-era look, with arched corridors and large halls.
