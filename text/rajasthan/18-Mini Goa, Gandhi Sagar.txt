Mini Goa: A Lakeside Escape at Gandhi Sagar
Location: Mini Goa, Gandhi Sagar Place: Bhanpura Genre: View Point
Known locally as Mini Goa, this stretch of the Gandhi Sagar shoreline is valued for its open water views and beach-like
atmosphere. It offers a relaxed contrast to the journey’s forts and temple complexes.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Reservoir shoreline, open landscape, scenic views and photography.
