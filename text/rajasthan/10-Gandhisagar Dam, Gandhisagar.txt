description: Gandhi Sagar Dam lies on the Chambal in the Mandsaur district of Madhya Pradesh, near the Rajasthan border. Completed around 1960, it was the first of the major dams in the Chambal Valley Project and impounds one of India's larger reservoirs. The long masonry wall, spillway gates and power station are the main sights, and the expanse of the lake behind, dotted with islands, is the real draw.
