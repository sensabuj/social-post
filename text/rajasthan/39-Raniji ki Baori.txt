Raniji ki Baori: The Queen’s Stepwell of Bundi
Location: Raniji ki Baori Place: Bundi Genre: Architectural
Raniji ki Baori, built in 1699 by Rani Nathavati Ji, is one of Bundi’s most celebrated stepwells. Its multi-storeyed
structure, ornate pillars and sculptural decoration reveal the sophistication of traditional water architecture and the role
of royal women in public works.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Arched entrances, ornate columns, carved brackets, deep stairways and
multi-level design.
