Taragarh Fort: The Guardian Above Bundi
Location: Taragarh Fort Place: Bundi Genre: Fort
Taragarh Fort rises above Bundi and dominates the town’s historic skyline. Its fortifications, gateways and elevated
position reflect the strategic importance of hilltop strongholds in Rajasthan. The fort also offers sweeping views across
Bundi’s blue-painted houses and surrounding hills.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Massive walls, gateways, reservoirs, palace remains and panoramic town
views.
