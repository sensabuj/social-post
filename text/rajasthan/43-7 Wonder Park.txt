Seven Wonders Park: Kota’s Modern Waterfront Attraction
Location: 7 Wonder Park Place: Kota Genre: Park
Seven Wonders Park is a contemporary recreational space in Kota, known for miniature replicas of famous world
monuments. It provides a modern, family-friendly contrast to the region’s historic palaces, temples and forts.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Monument replicas, landscaped pathways, lakefront setting and evening
views.
