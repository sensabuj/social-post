Holkar Chhatri: A Memorial to Royal Memory
Location: Maharaja Yashwant Rao Holkar Chhatri Place: Bhanpura Genre: Architectural
The chhatri at Bhanpura belongs to a regional tradition of memorial architecture that honours rulers and notable
figures. Its pavilion form turns remembrance into an architectural expression, linking the site to the Holkar legacy.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Domed canopy, supporting pillars, open pavilion form and memorial
setting.
