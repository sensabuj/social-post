Stone Remains of Artu: Reading History in the Ruins
Location: Temple Ruin Place: Artu Genre: Architectural
The temple ruins recorded at Artu offer a glimpse into the region’s dispersed architectural heritage. Even fragmentary
remains can reveal the layout, materials and decorative vocabulary of earlier sacred structures.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Surviving stonework, architectural fragments and the historical landscape.
