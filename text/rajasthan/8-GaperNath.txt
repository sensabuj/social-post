Article Title: GaperNath: A Shiva Shrine in a Dramatic Gorge

GaperNath, also called Gaipernath, is a Shiva shrine set amid a deep gorge and rugged natural scenery near Kota. The combination of rock faces, greenery and seasonal water makes it a memorable landscape destination. The itinerary notes that the site was closed to visitors during the planned visit.

Attractions and architectural highlights: Gorge scenery, rocky cliffs, waterfall surroundings and the sacred setting. Visitor access should be checked before travelling.
