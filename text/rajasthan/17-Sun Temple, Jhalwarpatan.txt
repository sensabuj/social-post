description: The Sun Temple (Surya or Padmanabha Temple) stands in the middle of Jhalrapatan, the old 'city of bells' in Jhalawar district, Rajasthan. Built around the 10th century in the Nagara style, it has a tall, finely carved shikhara, ornate pillars and sculpted panels of deities, apsaras and celestial figures. The grace of its carving has often drawn comparison with the temples of central India.
