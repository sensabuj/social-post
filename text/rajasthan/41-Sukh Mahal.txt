Sukh Mahal: A Palace by the Water
Location: Sukh Mahal Place: Bundi Genre: Architectural
Sukh Mahal is a historic palace beside Jait Sagar Lake in Bundi, associated with the rulers’ leisure and retreat. Its
setting and elegant proportions connect royal architecture with the surrounding lake landscape.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Palace façade, balconies, lake views and the relationship between
architecture and water.
