Bundi’s 84-Pillared Cenotaph: A Monument of Memory
Location: 84 Pillared Cenotaph Place: Bundi Genre: Architectural
The 84-pillared cenotaph, also known as Chaurasi Khambon ki Chhatri, is a distinctive memorial monument in Bundi.
Its forest of columns supports a domed canopy, creating a rhythmic architectural composition that honours the past
through stone.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Pillared hall, domed canopy, carved columns and memorial architecture.
