Article Title: Baroli Temples: The Stone Poetry of Ancient Rajasthan

The Baroli temple group near Rawatbhata is generally dated to the early medieval period and is associated with the region’s rich temple-building traditions. The complex reveals the skill of artisans who transformed stone into sacred architecture.

Attractions and architectural highlights: Ghateshwar Mahadev, carved pillars, sculpted doorways, temple towers and detailed stonework.
