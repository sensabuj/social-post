Kakuni: Echoes of an Ancient Sacred City
Location: Kakuni Temple Complex Place: Kakuni Genre: Architectural
Beside the Parvan River, Kakuni preserves remains associated with Hindu and Jain temple traditions. Though many
structures survive only as fragments, the site offers a glimpse of early medieval religious life and stone craftsmanship
in the Baran region.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Temple remains, carved stone fragments, sculptural details and riverside
setting.
