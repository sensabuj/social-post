Jhalrapatan Sun Temple: The Towering Artistry of the 10th Century
Location: Sun Temple Place: Jhalwarpatan Genre: Architectural
Jhalrapatan’s celebrated Sun Temple, also known as Padmanabha Temple, is a major example of early medieval
temple architecture. Its soaring shikhara and richly carved stone surfaces reflect the artistic ambition of its builders.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Ornate shikhara, sculpted pillars, carved entrances and intricate exterior
details.
