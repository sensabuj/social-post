description: Kishor Sagar Lake lies in the heart of old Kota, close to Garh Palace. The artificial lake is traditionally credited to the Bundi chieftain Dheer Deb in the 14th century. At its centre stands Jag Mandir, a small island palace raised in the 18th century for a Kota queen, with sandstone chhatris and arched pavilions. The lake is lovely at sunset, when the palace seems to float on the water.
