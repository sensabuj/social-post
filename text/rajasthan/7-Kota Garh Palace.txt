Article Title: Kota Garh Palace: A Royal Legacy Carved in Stone

Kota Garh Palace, also known as the City Palace, developed over generations of Hada rulers. Its fortified setting and layered buildings preserve the memory of Kota’s princely court and its artistic patronage.

Attractions and architectural highlights: Fortified gateways, courtyards, balconies, mural decoration and Rajput palace architecture.
