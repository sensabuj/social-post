Mini Khajuraho of Bansthuni: Sculpture in a Quiet Village
Location: Mini Khajuraho Temple Place: Bansthuni Genre: Architectural
The temple site at Bansthuni is popularly associated with the name ‘Mini Khajuraho,’ a reference to its sculptural
character. The comparison draws attention to the role of carved figures and ornament in regional temple art, while the
village setting gives the site its own distinct atmosphere.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Sculptural panels, carved figures, temple remains and rural heritage.
