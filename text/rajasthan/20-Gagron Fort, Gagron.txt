description: Gagron Fort stands near Jhalawar, Rajasthan, at the confluence of the Kali Sindh and Ahu rivers. It is a rare hill-and-water fort, ringed by rivers on three sides, with massive ramparts, bastions and gateways. Its origins go back to the early medieval period, and it passed through several dynasties, including the Dodiyas and Khichi Chauhans. It was inscribed in 2013 as part of the UNESCO 'Hill Forts of Rajasthan'.
