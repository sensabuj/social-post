Description: Rana Pratap Sagar Dam is a major masonry dam across the Chambal near Rawatbhata, Rajasthan, completed around 1970 as part of the Chambal Valley Project. It forms a large reservoir that supplies hydropower and irrigation and also supports the nearby Rawatbhata atomic power station. The straight dam wall, spillway gates and wide water spread make a striking sight, named in honour of the Mewar hero Maharana Pratap.

Description: Built across the Chambal River near Rawatbhata in Rajasthan, the Rana Pratap Sagar Dam is a monumental feat of post-independence engineering completed in the 1970s. Important attractions include the massive concrete gravity dam structure, panoramic viewpoints overlooking vast azure reservoirs, and adjacent hydroelectric power installations. The site highlights modern utilitarian architecture set against the rugged backdrop of the Vindhyan range.
