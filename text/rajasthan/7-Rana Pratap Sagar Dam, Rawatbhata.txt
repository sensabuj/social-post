description: Rana Pratap Sagar Dam is a major masonry dam across the Chambal near Rawatbhata, Rajasthan, completed around 1970 as part of the Chambal Valley Project. It forms a large reservoir that supplies hydropower and irrigation and also supports the nearby Rawatbhata atomic power station. The straight dam wall, spillway gates and wide water spread make a striking sight, named in honour of the Mewar hero Maharana Pratap.
