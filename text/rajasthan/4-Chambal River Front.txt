Article Title: Kota’s Riverfront: A New Chapter Along the Chambal

The Chambal Riverfront presents the city’s historic relationship with the river through a contemporary public landscape. Promenades, themed ghats, sculptural features and open views create a place to experience Kota beyond its palace walls.

Attractions and architectural highlights: Explore the river-facing ghats, architectural details, public art and changing light along the water.
