Bhimlat Waterfalls: A Green Oasis in Rajasthan
Location: Bhimlat Waterfalls Place: Bhimlat Genre: Waterfall
Bhimlat Waterfalls is a striking natural attraction in the Bundi region. Water descends through a rocky landscape,
creating a refreshing contrast with Rajasthan’s drier surroundings. Seasonal flow and greenery make the site
particularly appealing for nature photography.
Attractions and architectural highlights: Waterfall, rocky gorge, vegetation and scenic viewpoints. Exercise caution
around slippery edges.
