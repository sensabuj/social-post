description: Chuliya Waterfall is a seasonal cascade in the Chambal valley, in the Gandhisagar side of the itinerary. A small river drops over a rocky ledge into a gorge of dark stone, and the best months are during and just after the monsoon, when the flow is full. There is no grand architecture here, just a dramatic natural amphitheatre of rock, spray and green, which makes it a popular spot for photographers and families.
